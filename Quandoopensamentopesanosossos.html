Inês Sá

Quando o pensamento pesa nos ossos

In Quando o pensamento pesa nos ossos, we follow an intimate journey of growth shaped by contrasts: light days that seem to fly by and others when everything feels too heavy to carry. Through silence, memories and small fragments of everyday life, the book explores the emotions that shape who we become as we grow. There is no straight path, only different phases, setbacks and moments of clarity. It is a sensitive reflection on feeling deeply, learning to cope with pain and discovering the things that bring us comfort and hope. A space where vulnerability is not hidden, but embraced as an inevitable part of being human.
